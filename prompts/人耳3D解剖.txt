Build me a human ear in interactive 3D, in one HTML file with Three.js.
Make it look real: the outer ear and the canal, the eardrum stretched across the end of it, and behind it the parts you never get to see. The three little bones — malleus, incus, stapes — levering in the air-filled middle cavity, the oval window they push on, the Eustachian tube, and the cochlea coiled up like a snail shell. Uncoil the cochlea into a straight tapered tube so I can see the three fluid-filled chambers running its length, the basilar membrane dividing them, sitting on it the organ of Corti with its rows of hair cells, the tectorial膜 above their stereocilia, and the auditory nerve leaving from along the whole length.
Sound comes down the canal and moves the eardrum, the bones amplify it and the stapes pumps the oval window, and the fluid starts a travelling wave along the basilar membrane. The wave swells at one place and dies out after it: high notes peak near the base where the membrane is narrow and stiff, low notes near the apex where it is wide and floppy. At that peak the stereocilia bend, the channels open and a pulse runs up the auditory nerve. Play one tone about once a second and let me change its pitch so I can watch the peak slide up and down the membrane. I can drag to orbit around it and scroll to zoom into a single hair cell.
Add an Explode button. When I press it, every part slides apart in layers with its name on a label. Press it again and it all snaps back together. When I hover a part, it lights up and shows one line on what it does.
No images. Do not ask me any questions. Just build it.
