Build me a lockstitch sewing machine in interactive 3D, in one HTML file with Three.js.
Make it look real: the cast iron body with its arm and its flat bed, the handwheel on the right, the belt running down to the treadle, the needle bar and the presser foot, the spool of thread on top, and inside it the parts you never get to see. The feed dog under the needle plate, the rotating hook and the bobbin case down in the bed with the lower thread wound on the bobbin, the take-up lever up in the arm, the tension discs, and the thread guides all along the path. Two scraps of cloth on the bed, with the seam half finished so I can see the stitches.
The needle drives the upper thread down through the cloth, then rises a little — and that small rise is the whole trick: the thread lags behind and bulges into a loop on the underside of the needle. The hook comes round, catches that loop and carries it all the way around the bobbin case, then the take-up lever pulls up and tightens the knot in the middle of the cloth, and the feed dog lifts and pushes the fabric forward exactly one stitch. It sews about three stitches a second. I can drag to orbit around it and scroll to zoom under the needle plate.
Add an Explode button. When I press it, every part slides apart in layers with its name on a label. Press it again and it all snaps back together. When I hover a part, it lights up and shows one line on what it does.
No images. Do not ask me any questions. Just build it.
