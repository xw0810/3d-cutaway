Build me a hard disk drive in interactive 3D, in one HTML file with Three.js.
Make it look real: the sealed aluminium casing with its lid and screws, the label on top, the SATA contacts underneath, and inside it the parts you never get to see. The stack of platters on the spindle motor, the actuator arm swinging on its pivot, the voice coil motor that drives it, the suspension and the slider at the tip with the read and write head, the flex cable curling to the side, the ramp the arm parks on, the tiny breather hole and filter, and the controller board on the underside. Cut a wedge out of the top platter so I can see the tracks, the sectors, and the magnetic grains below the surface.
The platters spin about once a second so I can follow one sector going under the head. The voice coil swings the arm in a fast arc to a new track, settles, and the head floats a few nanometres above the surface on a cushion of air without ever touching it. At the moment it passes, the write head flips the grains under it and the read head senses their direction. One seek runs about once a second. I can drag to orbit around it and scroll to zoom down from the whole drive to the magnetic grains.
Add an Explode button. When I press it, every part slides apart in layers with its name on a label. Press it again and it all snaps back together. When I hover a part, it lights up and shows one line on what it does.
No images. Do not ask me any questions. Just build it.
